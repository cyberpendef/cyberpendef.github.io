## The Persistent Threat of Phishing Attacks

Phishing is not just a technical problem; it's a human one. Despite advanced filters, attackers constantly evolve their tactics to bypass security controls and exploit human psychology.

### Common Phishing Indicators

When reviewing an email, look for these common red flags:

1. **Urgency:** The message creates a sense of panic, demanding immediate action.
2. **Generic Greetings:** "Dear Valued Customer" instead of your actual name.
3. **Suspicious Links:** Hover over links to see the actual destination URL.
4. **Mismatched From Addresses:** The display name doesn't match the actual email address.

> "Security is a process, not a product. Phishing defense requires constant vigilance and education."

### Technical Defenses You Can Implement

While education is key, technical controls can significantly reduce your risk:

* **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA):** The single most effective technical control against credential theft.
* **Email Filtering:** Use modern email providers with advanced threat protection.
* **DMARC/SPF/DKIM:** Ensure your own domain is protected against spoofing.
